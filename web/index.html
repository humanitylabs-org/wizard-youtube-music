Home relay for cloud Wizards

On. YouTube goes through your home computer.

On an always-on computer at home that’s on your Tailscale, run:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/humanitylabs-org/wizard-home-relay/main/install.sh | sh

Then paste the address it prints.

Music finds this connector automatically. Search, radio and songs from YouTube show up there.
