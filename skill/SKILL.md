name: wizard-youtube-music
description: Use when YouTube Music won't play in Music, or to check or set up the YouTube Music connector.
---
The installed YouTube Music connector supplies Music via contract wizard.music-source/1. Call youtube_music_status for separate playback and account status; youtube_music_test tests a real download, and youtube_music_search searches the catalog. Account connection and guided home/VPS relay help are in the connector screen; owner OAuth prerequisites and limitations are in its README. Do not claim that stored credentials are verified, send passwords/cookies/codes through chat, or promise ad-free, unlimited, Premium or risk-free playback. The setup help request is owner-sent, not a delivered notification.
